One line roasting a smart fridge that refuses to open after midnight. The program validates the line against the bounty gate (1–200 characters) and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

The line:

> The fridge locked itself at midnight and left a note: 'I keep your food cold, not your 2 a.m. decisions — the leftovers have filed a restraining order.'

A fridge that treats midnight snacking as a legal matter: it keeps the food cold, refuses to enable the 2 a.m. decisions, and lets the leftovers do the talking.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It prints the line with its character count and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
